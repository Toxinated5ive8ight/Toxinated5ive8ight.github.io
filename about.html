About TechPulse

TechPulse is an independent technology and gaming information website focused on practical, easy-to-follow guidance.

What we publish

We create tutorials and explainers about Android devices, mobile gaming, apps and everyday digital safety. Our goal is to make technical topics easier to understand and help readers make informed choices.

Our editorial principles

  • Prioritise useful, original explanations.
  • Use clear steps and explain important limitations.
  • Avoid exaggerated claims and misleading headlines.
  • Update or correct information when we find an error.

TechPulse is a new publication. We will continue improving the site and adding carefully researched articles.
