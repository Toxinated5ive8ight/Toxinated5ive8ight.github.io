How to Master Weapons Fast in COD Mobile

Build consistent recoil control, tune Gunsmith attachments for your playstyle, and grind Weapon Mastery points efficiently.

Back to articles

1. Learn the Recoil Pattern in the Practice Range

Fire a full magazine at a wall in the Training/Practice Range without touching your aim first to see whether the gun kicks mostly straight up or pulls left or right. Pull smoothly in the opposite direction while firing.

2. Balance Mobility, Accuracy, and Control

Avoid stacking attachments that destroy ADS speed or movement speed unless you are holding long angles. Aim for a build that keeps bullet spread tight while letting you strafe and win close-to-mid-range duels.

3. Grind Mastery Points in the Right Modes

Points up to 2,000 can be earned in standard or ranked play, while higher Master progress (2,000 to 3,500+) requires Ranked Multiplayer (Pro and above) or Battle Royale. Pick modes where you get frequent kills and consistent match wins.

4. Burst or Tap at Long Range

Holding full-auto across long distances widens recoil and bullet spread. Short controlled bursts keep shots accurate and save ammo between targets.

Pro Tip: Stick to one primary weapon for several matches in a row so your muscle memory for its ADS time and recoil rhythm locks in.
