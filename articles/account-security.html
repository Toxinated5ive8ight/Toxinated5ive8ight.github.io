Simple Steps to Secure Your Online Accounts

By TechPulse Editorial Team · 5 minute read

Your online accounts contain important information. Protecting them with good security habits can reduce the risk of unauthorised access.

1. Use Strong Passwords

Create long, unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using the same password across multiple websites.

2. Enable Two-Step Verification

Two-step verification adds another security check when signing in. Enable it wherever the service offers this feature.

3. Protect Your Email Account

Your email is often connected to password recovery for other accounts. Use a strong password and enable additional security.

4. Avoid Suspicious Links

Be careful with unexpected messages asking you to sign in, claim prizes or provide personal information. Visit official websites directly instead.

5. Review Your Account Activity

Check the devices signed into your accounts. If you notice an unfamiliar session, use the service's official security guidance.

Final Thoughts

Online security starts with simple habits. Use unique passwords, enable two-step verification and stay alert when receiving unexpected messages.
